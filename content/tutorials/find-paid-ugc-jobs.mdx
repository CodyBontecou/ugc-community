## Where can you find paid UGC jobs?

You can find paid UGC jobs on public creator job boards, brand career pages, agency casting calls, and through direct outreach. Start with a written brief that states compensation, deliverables, eligibility, and an application destination. A request for content is not automatically a paid opportunity: affiliate commissions, performance bonuses, and free products have different payment terms.

[ugc.community](/jobs) is a public UGC job board that brings reviewed external opportunities and directly submitted brand briefs into one searchable feed. Creators can browse without an account or a subscription. Use the [guaranteed cash filter](/jobs?pay=cash) to look for listings categorized as cash compensation, then read the full terms before applying. Available roles change; a search may have no current matches.

## Which discovery channel fits your search?

| Channel | Useful when | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public UGC job board | You want to compare several briefs in one place | Source, closing date, compensation category, and original application link |
| Brand career or creator page | You already know which brand you want to work with | Whether the role involves creating assets, posting to your audience, or both |
| Agency casting call | You want a specific campaign or production role | The hiring agency, client, location, schedule, and payment responsibility |
| Direct outreach | Your samples match a brand but no suitable brief is public | Whether the brand is accepting pitches and who manages creator work |

These are complementary routes. A job board helps with discovery; the hiring party still decides whom to hire and on what terms. This guide is published by ugc.community and describes its own board alongside other ways to find work.

## How do you search ugc.community?

1. Open the [public jobs feed](/jobs). Try a product category such as skincare, food, or fitness using the keyword search.
2. Compare guaranteed cash listings with [base plus variable pay](/jobs?pay=mixed) if both fit your goals. Keep the fixed amount separate from possible bonuses.
3. Read deliverables, eligibility, usage rights, and closing dates. Treat any missing field as a question for the hiring party.
4. Follow the listing's application route. Some jobs accept email applications through the site; others send you to the original destination.

A creator account lets you save a [reusable application profile](/apply-profile) with work links and use it for jobs that accept email applications. Browsing does not require signing in.

## What should you compare before applying?

Record the same fields for every opportunity:

- **Guaranteed payment:** the fixed amount, currency, payment trigger, and expected payment date.
- **Deliverables:** video count, duration, aspect ratio, raw footage, hooks, and revision rounds.
- **Eligibility:** country, language, age requirements, shipping availability, and filming location.
- **Usage:** where the brand wants to publish the work, whether it includes ads, and the requested duration.
- **Application:** the destination, requested samples, deadline, and hiring contact.

A larger headline payment may cover more work or broader usage. Compare the scope before deciding which brief fits. Use the [compensation comparison guide](/tutorials/compare-ugc-compensation) for an example that separates fixed pay from variable earnings.

## How are listings reviewed and kept current?

ugc.community reviews listings before publication. External opportunities identify their source and link to the original destination. Missing terms are marked as not stated. Listings honor their closing dates; external opportunities also expire seven days after their last manual freshness review unless reviewed again.

Review does not guarantee employer legitimacy, payment, acceptance, or that a role remains open at the original source. Confirm the brief and application destination before sharing personal information. Send listing corrections to the [support contact](/contact).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is ugc.community free for creators?

Creators can browse without an account or subscription. Brands can submit jobs for free during launch. An account is required for personal creator tools and job submissions. See [About ugc.community](/about) for the current service description.

### Does every UGC job pay cash?

No. The board separates guaranteed cash, base plus variable pay, performance only, affiliate, gifted or unpaid, and unspecified compensation. A product or possible commission is not a guaranteed cash fee.

### Do I need followers to apply?

Check each brief. Creating videos for a brand's channels is different from distributing videos to your own audience. Some roles include audience requirements or posting commitments; others focus on samples and production ability. Do not assume either requirement is absent.

### Can an AI assistant search the board?

The [public API, MCP server, and CLI](/docs) can read current public jobs without an account or API key. They cannot submit applications or contact employers. The [agent guide](/guide) explains filters, attribution, and how to interpret listings.
