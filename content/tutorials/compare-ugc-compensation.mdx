## How do you compare two UGC job offers?

Compare the guaranteed cash amount against the full workload, payment conditions, and requested usage. Keep potential bonuses and commissions separate from fixed payment. The number of videos alone does not capture research, scripting, revisions, travel, raw footage, or posting obligations.

[ugc.community](/jobs) labels compensation so creators can distinguish fixed payment from variable earnings and product exchanges. These labels describe the stated payment structure. They do not establish a market rate, guarantee payment, or tell you whether an offer fits your needs.

## What do the compensation categories mean?

| Board category | How to read it | What to ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guaranteed cash | A stated cash fee for the agreed work | Amount, currency, acceptance criteria, and payment schedule |
| Base plus variable | A fixed amount plus potential additional earnings | Which amount is fixed and what triggers the rest |
| Performance only | Payment depends on stated performance conditions | Measurement window, qualifying events, reporting, and payout rules |
| Affiliate | Earnings depend on tracked referrals or sales | Attribution rules, commission basis, deductions, and payment timing |
| Gifted or unpaid | Product compensation or no stated cash payment | What is provided and whether content or posting is expected |
| Unspecified | The source does not state enough payment information | Ask for the payment structure before treating the role as paid |

The label “guaranteed cash” describes an agreed fee rather than earnings dependent on views or sales. It does not mean ugc.community guarantees the employer will pay. Review the original terms and who is responsible for payment.

## A worked comparison of two hypothetical offers

The following numbers are illustrative arithmetic, not observed creator earnings, recommended rates, or live jobs.

| Term | Offer A | Offer B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed cash | $240 | $360 |
| Finished videos | 2 | 4 |
| Included revisions | One round per video | Two rounds per video |
| Other deliverables | None in this example | Raw footage |
| Potential bonus | None | $120 if stated performance conditions are met |
| Fixed cash per finished video | $120 | $90 |

Offer B has the higher headline fixed payment, but it covers more videos, additional revisions, and raw footage. Its bonus is conditional. Offer A has more fixed cash per finished video in this example, but neither calculation establishes which is better without the usage terms, expected hours, and payment conditions.

To compare your own workload, estimate hours for planning, filming, editing, communication, and revisions. Divide the fixed fee by those estimated hours as a planning aid. Keep expenses and any additional obligations visible rather than treating that calculation as take home earnings.

## Which scope details change the comparison?

Ask the hiring party to specify:

- **Production:** finished video count, duration, formats, hook variations, reshoots, and raw footage.
- **Revisions:** included rounds, what counts as a revision, and what happens when the brief changes.
- **Usage:** organic channels, paid ads, duration, territories, and whether the brand needs advertising permissions through your account.
- **Distribution:** whether you must post to your own channels and keep content live for a set period.
- **Exclusivity:** any limits on working with other brands and how long they last.
- **Payment:** payer, currency, invoicing process, payment trigger, and timing.

These are questions for defining the offer, not assumptions about rights or contract enforceability. If a listing says “not stated,” the board has not supplied a missing term on the employer's behalf.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a good UGC rate?

There is no universal rate established by this guide. Define your production scope, expected hours, expenses, requested usage, and payment conditions before deciding what fee you can accept. A single per video number can hide substantial differences between briefs.

### Is gifted UGC a paid job?

Product compensation is different from a guaranteed cash fee. On ugc.community, gifted or unpaid opportunities have their own category so creators can assess them separately from cash work.

### Does a performance bonus count as guaranteed pay?

No. Count the fixed amount separately and treat a bonus as conditional on the written rules. If there is no fixed amount, do not present possible performance earnings as guaranteed cash.

### What should a brand include in its brief?

State compensation structure, deliverables, eligibility, usage, the application destination, and a closing date. Use the [UGC job brief template](/tutorials/ugc-job-brief-template) to make those terms easier to compare.
