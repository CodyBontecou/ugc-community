Creating great content is step one. Getting it *found* is step two. Discoverability is the art and science of making sure the right people see your videos — not just your followers, but the millions of potential viewers who are searching, scrolling, and discovering new creators every day.

This guide covers hashtags, captions, trending audio, TikTok SEO, and cross-platform strategies.

## Hashtag Strategy

Hashtags are still one of the primary ways platforms categorize and distribute your content. But there's a big difference between throwing random hashtags on a post and having a real strategy.

### How hashtags actually work

When you add a hashtag, you're essentially telling the platform: *"Put my video in this category."* The algorithm then shows your video to people who have engaged with content in that category.

The right hashtags = showing your video to people who are *already interested* in your topic.
The wrong hashtags = showing your video to people who will scroll right past it.

### The tiered hashtag approach

Use a mix of hashtag sizes to maximize reach:

| Tier | Size | Purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Big | 1M+ posts | Broad reach, high competition | #skincare, #fitness, #cooking |
| Medium | 100K–1M posts | Targeted reach, moderate competition | #skincareforbeginners, #homeworkout |
| Small/Niche | 10K–100K posts | Highly targeted, lower competition | #drugstoreskincare, #5minutemeals |
| Micro | Under 10K posts | Very specific, almost guaranteed visibility | #morningskincareroutine2026 |

### The formula

For each post, use **5–7 hashtags** with this mix:

- 1–2 big hashtags (reach)
- 2–3 medium hashtags (targeting)
- 2 small/niche hashtags (precision)

<Callout variant="tip">
More isn't better. Using 20+ hashtags looks spammy and can actually reduce your reach. Stick to 5–7 highly relevant tags. Quality over quantity, always.
</Callout>

### How to research hashtags

<Step number={1} title="Start with your niche keyword">
Type your main keyword into TikTok/Instagram search. Look at the suggested hashtags that appear — these are what people are actively searching for.
</Step>

<Step number={2} title="Check competitor content">
Look at the top-performing videos in your niche. What hashtags are they using? These are proven to work in your space.
</Step>

<Step number={3} title="Build a hashtag bank">
Create a note on your phone with 20–30 relevant hashtags organized by tier. For each post, you'll pick 5–7 from this bank instead of brainstorming from scratch.
</Step>

### Hashtags to avoid

- **Banned or flagged hashtags** — Some hashtags are shadowbanned. If you notice a sudden drop in reach, try removing a hashtag and reposting.
- **Completely unrelated hashtags** — Using #viral or #foryou doesn't help if your content has nothing to do with those broad categories. (That said, on TikTok, #fyp is still commonly used as a discovery tag.)
- **Brand-specific hashtags you're not affiliated with** — Don't tag a brand in your post unless you're actually working with them or reviewing their product.

## Caption Strategy

Captions aren't just descriptions — they're a discoverability tool. Platforms use the text in your captions to understand what your content is about and who to show it to.

### Keyword-rich captions

Write captions that naturally include terms people search for:

> ❌ *"Try this!! 😍"*

> ✅ *"My morning skincare routine using only drugstore products — SPF included ☀️"*

The second caption tells the algorithm exactly what the video is about. Anyone searching for "morning skincare routine" or "drugstore skincare" might discover your content.

### Caption formulas

**The Keyword-Loaded:**
> *"[Primary keyword] that [benefit] — perfect for [target audience]"*
> Example: "Quick meal prep ideas that actually taste good — perfect for busy weekdays"

**The Story Tease:**
> *"I've been using [product/method] for [time period] and [result]... [call to action]"*
> Example: "I've been using this planner for 3 months and my productivity doubled... watch till the end to see my setup"

**The Question:**
> *"Has anyone else tried [product/method]? Here's what happened when I did 👇"*
> Example: "Has anyone else tried ice rolling? Here's what happened when I did 👇"

### The brand name trick

<Callout variant="tip">
When creating UGC for a brand, **don't mention the brand name in your caption.** Instead, put it in the comments. This creates a natural engagement loop — people see your content, get curious, and either ask "What is that?" or find the answer in the comments. This approach makes the content feel more organic and less like an ad, which helps it perform better algorithmically.
</Callout>

## Trending Audio Strategy

Audio is a major factor in how platforms distribute your content. Using the right audio at the right time can significantly boost your reach.

### How to find trending sounds

- **Browse your FYP.** When you notice the same sound appearing in multiple videos, it's trending.
- **Check the sounds library.** TikTok's sound library has a "trending" section that shows what's popular right now.
- **Follow trend-tracking accounts.** Several creators specialize in posting trending sounds before they peak.
- **Look at the arrow icon.** On TikTok, trending sounds show a small arrow icon next to the sound name.

### When to use trending audio

**Use trending audio when:**
- The sound matches your content's mood or message
- You're creating a trend-based video (POV, transition, etc.)
- You want maximum algorithmic boost
- The sound is on the way up (early adoption gets the most benefit)

**Use original audio when:**
- You're doing a voiceover or tutorial
- The content relies on what you're saying
- There's no trending sound that fits naturally
- You're creating a product demo where the viewer needs to hear you speak

### Matching audio to content type

| Content Type | Best Audio |
|---|---|
| Product demo with explanation | Original voiceover |
| Before/after transformation | Trending audio (dramatic/upbeat) |
| POV / relatable content | Trending audio (relevant to the vibe) |
| Tutorial / how-to | Original voiceover or calm background music |
| Unboxing / first impressions | Original audio (genuine reactions) |
| Aesthetic / lifestyle | Trending audio (chill/moody) |

## TikTok SEO Basics

TikTok is increasingly becoming a *search engine*, especially for younger users. Optimizing your content for search (SEO) means your videos can be discovered through TikTok's search bar — not just the FYP.

### How TikTok SEO works

TikTok analyzes several signals to determine what your video is about:

- **On-screen text** — The words that appear in your video
- **Voiceover/speech** — What you say (TikTok transcribes audio)
- **Caption text** — Your written caption
- **Hashtags** — Topic categorization
- **Sounds** — What audio is associated with your content

### SEO optimization checklist

<Checklist>
  <ChecklistItem>Include your main keyword in the on-screen text</ChecklistItem>
  <ChecklistItem>Say the keyword out loud if doing a voiceover</ChecklistItem>
  <ChecklistItem>Use the keyword naturally in your caption</ChecklistItem>
  <ChecklistItem>Add 2–3 relevant hashtags that include the keyword or variations</ChecklistItem>
  <ChecklistItem>Use a descriptive title/first line that matches what people would search</ChecklistItem>
</Checklist>

### Example: SEO-optimized video

**Niche:** Skincare
**Target keyword:** "morning skincare routine"

- **On-screen text:** "My 5-minute morning skincare routine"
- **Voiceover:** "Here's my morning skincare routine that takes less than 5 minutes..."
- **Caption:** "The morning skincare routine that cleared my skin — all drugstore products ☀️"
- **Hashtags:** #morningskincare #skincareroutine #drugstoreskincare #skincaretips #spf

<Highlight emoji="🔍" title="Think like a searcher">
Before creating content, ask yourself: "What would someone type into the TikTok search bar to find this?" That phrase is your target keyword. Use it in your text, voiceover, and caption.
</Highlight>

## Cross-Platform Strategy

Creating content for multiple platforms doesn't mean starting from scratch each time. It means being smart about how you repurpose and distribute.

### The repurposing workflow

<Step number={1} title="Create for TikTok first">
TikTok is the most native format for short-form UGC. Create your video here first with no platform-specific watermarks.
</Step>

<Step number={2} title="Save the clean version">
Before posting, save the video to your camera roll *without* the TikTok watermark. You can also use your original recording before adding TikTok-specific effects.
</Step>

<Step number={3} title="Adapt captions and hashtags for each platform">
TikTok and Instagram have different hashtag cultures. Don't just copy-paste — adjust your caption and hashtags for each platform's norms.
</Step>

<Step number={4} title="Stagger your posts">
Don't post to all three platforms at the same time. Post to TikTok first, then Instagram Reels 4–6 hours later, then YouTube Shorts the next day.
</Step>

### Platform-specific adjustments

**TikTok → Instagram Reels:**
- Remove any TikTok watermark
- Slightly more polished caption
- Fewer hashtags (Instagram tends to use 5–10 vs TikTok's broader approach)
- Add to relevant Stories for extra reach

**TikTok → YouTube Shorts:**
- Remove TikTok watermark
- Write a clear, search-optimized title (YouTube is search-first)
- Add a description with keywords
- YouTube doesn't use hashtags the same way — focus on title and description

<Callout variant="warning">
All three platforms actively penalize content with competitor watermarks. A TikTok video with a visible TikTok watermark will get suppressed on Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Always save and repost the clean version.
</Callout>
