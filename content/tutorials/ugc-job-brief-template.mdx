## What should a UGC job brief include?

A UGC job brief should name the hiring business, describe the product and content needed, state the payment structure, list deliverables and eligibility, explain requested usage, and provide an application destination and closing date. Clear terms help creators assess whether they can do the work before preparing an application.

Brands can [submit a UGC job on ugc.community](/post-job) for free during launch after creating an account or signing in. Submissions are reviewed before publication. The board also includes reviewed external opportunities, labeled separately from jobs posted directly. Submission does not guarantee publication or a particular number of applicants.

## Copy this UGC job brief template

Replace every bracketed field with your actual terms. If a term is still undecided, say so and explain when it will be confirmed.

```text
Role title: [Product category and content format]
Hiring business: [Business name and public website]
Product or service: [What the creator will demonstrate]
Campaign goal: [What the content should communicate]

Deliverables: [Video count, duration, aspect ratios, hooks, raw footage]
Creator responsibilities: [Scripting, filming, editing, posting]
Timeline: [Product arrival, first draft, review, final delivery]
Revisions: [Included rounds and scope]

Compensation type: [Fixed cash, base plus variable, performance only,
                    affiliate, gifted or unpaid]
Fixed payment: [Amount and currency, or state that there is none]
Variable payment: [Conditions and calculation, or none]
Payment schedule: [Trigger, invoicing process, timing, payer]
Product and expenses: [What is supplied and who covers costs]

Eligibility: [Country, language, skills, location, audience requirements]
Usage requested: [Organic, ads, channels, territories, duration]
Posting requirements: [Creator channels and posting duration, or none]
Exclusivity: [Scope and duration, or none]

How to apply: [Form URL or designated application contact]
Application materials: [Samples and information needed to evaluate fit]
Application closing date: [Date and time zone]
Hiring contact: [Business contact for questions]
```

Use a working destination you control and request only the information needed to evaluate an application. Keep confidential campaign materials out of a public listing.

## An illustrative brief

This is a fictional example for formatting, not an open job or a statement about typical pay.

> We need two vertical product demonstration videos for a refillable kitchen spray. Each video should run 20 to 30 seconds and include scripting, filming, and editing. We will provide the product. The fixed fee is $280 USD for both videos, including one revision round per video. There is no performance bonus. We will pay within 14 calendar days after final delivery and receipt of an invoice. We request use on our brand's organic social channels for 90 days after delivery; paid ads, raw footage, creator posting, and exclusivity are outside this brief. Creators must be in the United States for product shipping and able to film in English. The first draft is due 10 days after product delivery.

Before publishing a brief like this, add your real business identity, application destination, hiring contact, and closing date. Confirm that the scope, usage, and payment language reflect the agreement your business intends to offer.

## How should you describe compensation?

Separate fixed payment from possible additional earnings. “$180 fixed fee plus a conditional $90 bonus” communicates more than “earn up to $270.” If compensation is commission only or product only, state that directly.

The board distinguishes guaranteed cash, base plus variable, performance only, affiliate, gifted or unpaid, and unspecified compensation. Read the [creator compensation guide](/tutorials/compare-ugc-compensation) to see how applicants may compare your offer with its workload.

## How does posting on ugc.community work?

1. Create an account or sign in and open [Post a job](/post-job).
2. Submit your business details, brief, compensation, eligibility, usage, application destination, and closing date.
3. The submission enters review before it can appear publicly. If you need to amend a submission, [contact support](/contact) with the company and role title.

Published listings honor their closing dates. Hiring teams manage selection and payment. A reviewed listing is not an endorsement of the employer or a guarantee of results.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is posting a UGC job free?

Job submissions are free during launch. An account is required, and submissions undergo review. This is the current launch policy rather than a promise of permanent pricing.

### Can I post an affiliate or gifted opportunity?

The board has separate compensation categories for affiliate and gifted or unpaid opportunities. Describe the arrangement accurately and submit it for review. Do not label products or potential commissions as guaranteed cash payment.

### Do creators have to post to their own audience?

Only include that requirement if your campaign needs it, and state the channels and duration. Creating assets for brand channels and posting to a creator's audience are different deliverables.

### Can I hire creators through the public API or MCP server?

The [public interfaces](/docs) only read current published jobs. They cannot submit briefs, access private applications, or contact creators. Use the website to submit a job.
