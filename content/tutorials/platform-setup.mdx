Your social media profiles are your digital storefront. Before you start creating content, you need to set them up correctly — not just create an account, but *optimize* it so that brands and the right audience can find you.

This guide covers the three major short-form platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

## TikTok Setup & Optimization

TikTok is the #1 platform for UGC right now. It's where brands look first when hiring creators, and it's where viral content happens most organically.

### Creating your account

If you're starting fresh (recommended for UGC-focused creators), here's how to do it right:

<Step number={1} title="Choose your username strategically">
Your username is one of the first things brands see. Pick something that signals what you do:

- **Niche-focused:** `skincarewithsam`, `fitfoodsby.ana`, `techreviewsjake`
- **Creator-brand:** `sam.creates`, `ana.ugc`, `jake.films`
- **Personal + niche:** `samantha.beauty`, `ana.wellness`

Avoid random numbers, underscores, or anything hard to spell. You want someone to be able to find you by searching your name.
</Step>

<Step number={2} title="Set up your bio">
Your bio has limited characters — make every word count. Use this formula:

**Line 1:** What you create → "UGC creator | Beauty & skincare content"
**Line 2:** Your vibe or social proof → "Making products look irresistible ✨"
**Line 3:** Call to action → Link to your creator profile or work samples

</Step>

<Step number={3} title="Profile photo">
Use a clear, well-lit headshot where your face is visible. No sunglasses, no heavy filters, no group photos. You want to look approachable and professional — this photo will appear on every video you post.
</Step>

<Step number={4} title="Switch to a Creator or Business account">
Go to Settings → Manage Account → Switch to Business Account. This gives you access to analytics and the ability to add a website link in your bio.
</Step>

<Callout variant="tip">
Use the same profile photo and display name across all platforms. Consistency builds recognition — if a brand finds you on TikTok and checks your Instagram, they should instantly know it's the same person.
</Callout>

### Bio formulas that convert

Here are plug-and-play bio formulas. Pick one and customize it:

**The Professional:**
```
UGC Creator | [Niche] Content
Helping brands tell authentic stories
📩 [email] | 🔗 portfolio link
```

**The Personality-Led:**
```
Making [niche] content that actually converts ✨
[Personality trait] creator who loves [interest]
DM for collabs 💌
```

**The Results-Focused:**
```
[Niche] UGC creator
Content that hooks, converts & goes viral
Open to brand partnerships 🤝
```

### Account hygiene

<Callout variant="warning">
New accounts are under extra scrutiny from TikTok's bot detection. Follow these rules to avoid getting flagged:

- **Don't follow more than 20–25 accounts per hour.** Spread follows throughout the day.
- **Don't like hundreds of videos in a row.** Mix likes with watching, commenting, and saving.
- **Don't use third-party automation tools.** They will get your account restricted or banned.
- **Post gradually.** Start with 1 video/day in your first week, then scale up to 2–3/day.
</Callout>

## Training Your Algorithm

This is the single most important thing you can do in your first week — and most people skip it entirely. Training your algorithm means teaching the platform what your content is about, so it knows *who to show your videos to*.

### The technique

For the first 3–5 days, spend 10–15 minutes daily doing this:

<Step number={1} title="Search niche keywords">
Go to the search bar and type terms related to your niche. If you're in fitness, search: "home workout," "gym routine," "protein shake recipe," "fitness transformation," etc.
</Step>

<Step number={2} title="Filter by recent">
Set the filter to "This week" to see fresh, trending content in your niche.
</Step>

<Step number={3} title="Engage deeply">
Don't just scroll. **Watch videos all the way through.** Like the good ones. Leave genuine comments (not just emojis — write something real). Save a few to your favorites.
</Step>

<Step number={4} title="Follow niche creators">
Follow 10–15 established creators in your niche. This tells the algorithm what community you belong to.
</Step>

<Step number={5} title="Check your For You Page">
Within 2–3 days, your FYP should start shifting toward your niche. When 80%+ of your feed is relevant content, your algorithm is trained.
</Step>

<Highlight emoji="🎯" title="Why this works">
TikTok's algorithm uses your engagement patterns to categorize your account. When you consistently interact with fitness content, TikTok thinks: "This is a fitness account." Then when you post fitness content, it gets pushed to fitness audiences — not random viewers.
</Highlight>

### Niche keyword lists by category

Here are starter keyword lists to search and engage with:

**Beauty / Skincare:** skincare routine, glass skin, morning skincare, best serums, skincare for beginners, acne treatment, SPF review, beauty haul

**Fitness:** home workout, gym motivation, protein recipe, workout routine, fitness transformation, weightlifting, yoga flow, HIIT workout

**Tech:** tech review, best gadgets, iPhone tips, productivity apps, desk setup, unboxing, tech haul, app review

**Food:** easy recipe, meal prep, cooking hack, what I eat in a day, healthy meals, budget cooking, food review, kitchen gadgets

**Lifestyle:** daily routine, morning routine, apartment tour, organization hack, self care, productivity tips, minimalism, haul

## Instagram Reels Setup

Instagram is slightly more polished than TikTok — and many brands use it to vet creators. Your Instagram profile should feel curated but authentic.

### Key differences from TikTok

- **Grid aesthetics matter more.** Your Reels appear on your profile grid. Think about visual consistency.
- **Stories are a bonus.** Use Stories to show behind-the-scenes and build personal connection.
- **Brands DM on Instagram more.** Keep your DMs open and check them regularly.
- **Hashtag strategy differs.** Instagram uses fewer but more targeted hashtags than TikTok.

### Setup checklist

<Checklist>
  <ChecklistItem>Switch to a Creator or Professional account (Settings → Account → Account type)</ChecklistItem>
  <ChecklistItem>Use the same username as TikTok (or as close as possible)</ChecklistItem>
  <ChecklistItem>Same profile photo and display name across platforms</ChecklistItem>
  <ChecklistItem>Write a bio optimized for brands: what you do + niche + contact method</ChecklistItem>
  <ChecklistItem>Add your creator profile or work samples link</ChecklistItem>
  <ChecklistItem>Set up your Highlights with categories: "UGC Work," "About Me," "Testimonials"</ChecklistItem>
  <ChecklistItem>Post your first 3–6 Reels before you start reaching out to brands</ChecklistItem>
</Checklist>

## YouTube Shorts Setup

YouTube Shorts is the fastest-growing short-form platform, with less competition for UGC creators. It's also great for long-term discoverability — YouTube content gets searched months and years later.

### Why it matters for UGC creators

- **Search-driven discovery.** People search YouTube for product reviews, tutorials, and recommendations. Your Shorts can rank for these searches.
- **Longer shelf life.** A TikTok might peak in 48 hours. A YouTube Short can get views for months.
- **Brand credibility.** Having a YouTube presence signals professionalism to brands.

### Setup checklist

<Checklist>
  <ChecklistItem>Create a YouTube channel with your creator name</ChecklistItem>
  <ChecklistItem>Upload a channel banner and profile photo</ChecklistItem>
  <ChecklistItem>Write a channel description with your niche and UGC focus</ChecklistItem>
  <ChecklistItem>Add your portfolio link in the About section</ChecklistItem>
  <ChecklistItem>Upload at least 3 Shorts before promoting your channel</ChecklistItem>
</Checklist>

<Callout variant="info">
You can cross-post TikTok content to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. Just make sure to remove any platform-specific watermarks first — all three platforms penalize content with competitor watermarks.
</Callout>

## Cross-Platform Strategy

You don't need to be on every platform from day one. Here's the recommended approach:

**Week 1–2:** Focus entirely on TikTok. Get your rhythm, train your algorithm, post consistently.

**Week 3–4:** Add Instagram Reels. Cross-post your best TikTok content (watermark-free) and start building your grid.

**Month 2+:** Add YouTube Shorts. Cross-post top performers and start optimizing titles for search.

<Callout variant="tip">
The goal isn't to be everywhere — it's to be *great* somewhere first, then expand. A strong TikTok profile with 20 solid videos will impress brands more than having 5 mediocre videos scattered across three platforms.
</Callout>
