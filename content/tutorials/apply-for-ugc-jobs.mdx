## What belongs in a UGC job application?

A useful UGC application contains relevant work samples, a short explanation of how you fit the brief, your availability, and any requested eligibility details. Follow the listing's instructions. If payment, deliverables, or usage are unclear, ask for those terms before agreeing to the project.

On [ugc.community](/jobs), creators can browse reviewed opportunities without signing in. An account lets you save a [reusable application profile](/apply-profile) and work links for jobs that accept email applications through the site. Other listings use the original application destination.

## Step 1: Choose a brief you can fulfill

Read the requirements before preparing a pitch. Check the product category, filming location, language, deadlines, deliverables, and any audience or posting requirements. A remote role can still have country restrictions for product shipping or campaign eligibility.

If you need fixed payment, start with [guaranteed cash opportunities](/jobs?pay=cash). Do not treat affiliate commissions, performance bonuses, or gifted products as a substitute for a stated cash fee. A listing with unspecified compensation needs clarification.

Use the [job discovery guide](/tutorials/find-paid-ugc-jobs) to compare opportunities across boards, brand pages, casting calls, and outreach.

## Step 2: Select samples that answer the brief

Choose a small set of relevant samples rather than sending every video you have made. A skincare demonstration, an app walkthrough, and a food preparation video show different skills. Match your examples to the requested format and explain your role in making them.

If you have no client work, you can create a practice sample using a product you already own. Label it as a practice or independently produced sample so the brand can distinguish it from commissioned work. Do not imply a past brand relationship or invent performance results.

For each sample, make it easy to see:

- The finished video through a working, viewable link.
- What you handled, such as scripting, filming, voiceover, or editing.
- Why it relates to the brief.
- Any results you can substantiate, if results are relevant and you have permission to share them.

Open your links in a signed out browser before submitting. A private file or expired sharing link prevents the hiring team from evaluating the work.

## Step 3: Write a short, specific application

Use this template as a starting point and replace the bracketed details:

> Hello [hiring contact], I am applying for [role or campaign]. I create [relevant format or subject], and these samples show [specific skill the brief requests]: [sample links]. I can deliver [requested scope] within [your actual availability]. I meet [relevant eligibility requirements]. Could you confirm [missing term]? Thank you, [name and preferred contact].

Keep claims factual. If the listing requests a rate, quote the scope your rate covers and identify anything that needs a separate agreement. If it requests a particular subject line or form, use it.

## Step 4: Use the correct application route

| Listing route | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Email application through ugc.community | Sign in, complete your reusable profile, and review the application before submitting |
| External form or website | Follow the original destination and its instructions |
| Terms or destination are unclear | Contact the hiring party through a verified channel or report the listing for review |

The public jobs API and MCP tools are for reading listings. They do not submit applications. Hiring teams control their own decisions and response times; applying through the site does not guarantee a reply.

## Step 5: Clarify the agreement before production

Ask for the final deliverables, revision scope, payment schedule, usage duration, and any exclusivity or posting requirements in writing. If the brand requests an unpaid custom sample, clarify what it can use and decide whether the work is appropriate for you before making it.

Use the [compensation checklist](/tutorials/compare-ugc-compensation) to compare the offer with the workload. Keep a record of the agreed scope and the application destination so you can follow up in context.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I apply without previous brand clients?

You can apply to briefs whose requirements you meet. Clearly labeled practice samples can demonstrate your skills, but the hiring party decides whether your experience fits. There is no guaranteed path from a first application to a paid project.

### Should I send the same application to every brand?

Reuse your accurate profile details and work links, then tailor the samples and explanation to each brief. An application for an app tutorial should explain relevant screen recording or demonstration skills rather than an unrelated product niche.

### Do I need to buy a course or equipment before applying?

ugc.community does not require a course purchase to browse jobs. Check each brief's production requirements and assess whether your current setup can meet them. Avoid buying equipment solely on the assumption that an application will be accepted.

### Where do I begin?

[Browse current opportunities](/jobs), pick a brief you can fulfill, and prepare the samples it asks for. Save your [application profile](/apply-profile) if you want to use email applications on supported listings.
